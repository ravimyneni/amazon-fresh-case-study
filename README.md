# Shop by Recipe for Amazon Fresh — UX case study

Case study and clickable prototype by **Ravi Myneni**, Senior Product / UX Designer.

- Case study: https://ravimyneni.github.io/amazon-fresh-case-study/
- Prototype: https://ravimyneni.github.io/amazon-fresh-case-study/walkthrough.html

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Final screens are WebP exports from Figma. Hotspots are defined in `screens.js`.

The case study page includes the research from the original project: the competitor comparison (also written as a table), the survey charts (also written as stats and a bar chart where the numbers are printed), three personas, the user flow, the information architecture, and the wireframes. Research images sit alongside the page as WebP files. Click a research image to open it full size.
